## Open evidence gaps

- Lenovo’s platform documents leave the X280 drive lengths and the optional T480 secondary drive length unspecified. Individual PSREF SKU pages for T480 and X280 were investigated but did not expose readable contents when opened for verification. They were omitted as evidence. These lengths stay unknown until exact documentation is verified.
- The X280 maintenance-manual PDF attempt returned inaccessible and the T480 maintenance-manual PDF exceeded the retrieval limit. The linked support pages remain useful for identification and finding the guides. Neither manual supplied any claim. The release includes no disassembly procedure.
- No source checked established an absolute storage capacity ceiling for any listed platform. The catalogue records listed capacities where documented and leaves the ceiling unknown.
- The published machine types are the source-checked set shown on each record. RAM and storage options vary by full MTM, region and current installed hardware. No physical laptop or part was independently tested.
